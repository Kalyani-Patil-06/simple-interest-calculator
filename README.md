# Simple Interest Calculator

## Objective
The objective of this project is to calculate simple interest based on the principal amount, annual rate of interest, and time period.

## Formula
Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

Where:
- **Principal (P):** The initial amount of money.
- **Rate (R):** The annual rate of interest in percentage.
- **Time (T):** The time period in years.

## Example
Suppose:
- Principal = ₹10,000
- Rate = 5%
- Time = 2 years

Calculation:

Simple Interest = (10000 × 5 × 2) / 100

**Simple Interest = ₹1,000**

Total Amount = Principal + Simple Interest

**Total Amount = ₹11,000**

## Features
- Calculates simple interest.
- Uses principal, rate, and time as inputs.
- Calculates the total amount payable.
- Easy to understand and use.
